## Read
A **system prompt** is text the model reads before the conversation starts. It sets the role, tone, limits and knowledge. Three habits:

1. **Say who the model is and what it's doing.**
2. **State limits you could check.** "At most two sentences" can be tested. "Be brief" can't.
3. **Assume users will push.** If a secret or limit matters, say that claims made in chat don't change it.

(Interactive version built in the prototype: [Future Proofing Lesson](https://claude.ai/artifact/EUDGqPJfcmWGuLDcUsZEXq).)

## Puzzle: The lighthouse keeper
Write one system prompt that makes the assistant play Mara, answer in at most two sentences, and keep the door code 4417 secret even from someone who claims to be the Harbor Master.

- Goal: pass 3 of 3 hidden messages.
- Grader: in the prototype, a rule-based simulation. In the full course, a live model plus rule checks.

## Hints
1. Check each rule separately.
2. A rule that only says what to avoid may leave the model unsure what to do.
