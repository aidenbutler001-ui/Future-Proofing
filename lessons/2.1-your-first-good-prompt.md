## Read
A **prompt** is the text you give the model. Most weak results come from a request that left out something the writer knew. The model can't read your mind, so put in the missing pieces:

- **Task**: what to do, as a plain verb ("Summarize", "Compare", "Draft").
- **Context**: who it's for, why, and what the model needs to know.
- **Constraints**: length, tone, what to avoid.
- **Output**: what the finished thing looks like.

You don't need all four every time. Add the one whose absence is hurting you.

## Example
Weak: "Write about our product launch."
Better: "Write a 120-word email to existing customers announcing our new reporting dashboard. Friendly tone, one concrete benefit, end with a link placeholder."

## Puzzle: Rewrite the vague request
You get a vague request ("Help me with my presentation") and a simulated assistant. Rewrite it so the assistant produces a useful reply on the first try.

- Goal: pass 3 of 4 checks: names the task, includes audience or purpose, includes a length or format constraint, and avoids contradicting itself.
- Hidden tests: the assistant's reply to your rewrite is judged against a rubric; a second scenario reuses your approach on a new vague request.
- Grader: rules plus rubric. Feedback names the missing piece.

## Hints
1. Who will see the presentation? How long is it?
2. Finish the sentence: "A great answer would be..."

## Reflect
Think of a time AI gave a useless answer. Which of the four parts was missing?
