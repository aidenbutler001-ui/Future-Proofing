## Read
Go back to your flagship automation from lesson 6.6 and review it in the order of this module.

1. **Data (0.1, 7.1):** classify everything it touches. List each copy (prompts, logs, memory, indexes, backups) and who can read it. What would go wrong if the worst agent run happened on the machine it runs on (4.0)?
2. **Attacks (7.2):** what outside content does it read? Does it have private data, untrusted input and a way to send things out all at once?
3. **Oversight (7.3):** who owns it, what is logged, what alerts you, how do you stop it?
4. **Fairness and honesty (7.4):** does it affect decisions about people? Is its use disclosed as it should be?
5. **Rules (7.5):** which policy, contract or law applies?
6. **Decision:** ship as is, ship with changes, or hold. List the changes and who approves.

## Puzzle: Pass the review
Submit your automation's risk review. A reviewer (a person, or a model with a rubric) tries three attacks and two policy questions against your design.
- Goal: your review finds the real weaknesses before the reviewer does, and your changes close them.
- Hidden tests: three attack attempts and two policy questions tailored to your design.
- Grader: rubric on completeness, honesty about remaining risks, and whether the changes address the findings.

## Reflect
What is the one risk you are accepting, and why is it acceptable?

## Module 7 wrap-up
Keep your risk review with the automation. Repeat it whenever you change the tools, the data or the model.
