## Read
"AI" is used for many different things. A useful first split is by what the model takes in and what it produces:

| Kind | Takes in | Produces | Everyday example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Language models (LLMs)** | Text (and often images or files) | Text, code | Chat assistants, writing and coding help |
| **Image generators** | A text description | Images | "A watercolor of a harbor at dusk" |
| **Speech models** | Audio, or text | Text transcripts, or spoken audio | Voice dictation, read-aloud |
| **Video and audio generators** | Text, images | Clips, music | Short generated videos |
| **Classifiers and predictors** | Data (numbers, images, records) | A label or score | Spam filter, fraud flag, photo tagging |
| **Recommenders** | Your history and others' | A ranked list | What to watch next |
| **Embedding models** | Text or images | A list of numbers that captures meaning | Search by meaning, "find similar" |

Two more distinctions:
- **General vs. specialized.** An LLM is a generalist you instruct in words. A fraud detector is a specialist trained for one job.
- **Multimodal.** Many modern assistants accept several kinds of input (text, images, audio) in one model. The boundaries between the rows above are blurring.

This course focuses on LLMs, because they are the most flexible and the ones people use directly. Image, video and audio models will arrive as add-on components later.

## Example
You photograph a receipt and ask an assistant to list the items. A speech model may transcribe your question, a vision-capable LLM reads the photo, and the LLM writes the list. One chat, several kinds of model.

## Puzzle: Pick the right tool
You get 8 short scenarios (e.g. "flag suspicious card payments", "summarize a 20-page report", "turn a meeting recording into notes"). For each, choose the kind of model that fits best, or two if the job needs a combination.

- Goal: 7 of 8 correct.
- Hidden tests: the 8 scenarios. Two accept more than one answer; one is a trap where an LLM is the wrong choice.
- Grader: answer key with accepted alternatives; feedback names the input and output of the right tool.
