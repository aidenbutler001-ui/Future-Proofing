## Read
Choose a real recurring task: sort and summarize incoming email, turn meeting notes into action items and calendar entries, track applications, prepare a weekly briefing. Deliver:

1. **Spec:** trigger, inputs, outputs, and what "done" and "wrong" look like.
2. **Shape:** workflow, agent, or a mix, and why the simpler options weren't enough (lesson 6.1).
3. **Build:** the steps, tools, credentials with least access, and rules for human approval.
4. **Reliability:** validation, retries, de-duplication, logging (lesson 6.4).
5. **Eval:** 10 test cases, including 3 that try to break it (lessons 2.6, 5.5).
6. **Numbers:** cost and time per run, and the quality rate on your tests (lesson 6.5).
7. **Plan:** who watches it, what alerts you, how you turn it off.

**Opsec check.** Before building, list every piece of confidential data the automation touches and where each copy ends up. Build it in an environment that can't reach anything else (lesson 4.0).

## Puzzle: Ship and defend it
Submit the package. A reviewer (a person, or a model with a rubric) runs your tests, tries three inputs you haven't seen, and asks you two questions about failure.
- Goal: it passes your tests, survives the reviewer's three inputs or documents the gaps, and the numbers hold up.
- Grader: rubric on fit of shape, safety, tests, numbers and honesty about limits.
