## Read
When a system answers from documents, you should be able to see where each claim came from. Build that in:

- **Ask for sources by name and location:** "Cite the document and section for each claim."
- **Ask for the exact quote,** then check it matches the text.
- **Require an honest "I don't know."** A good system says "the documents don't cover this" and does not guess.
- **Handle conflict.** If two sources disagree, the answer should say so, name both, and not pick quietly.

Check three things on every citation: does the source exist, does the quote appear in it, and does it actually support the claim? A citation can pass the first two and fail the third.

**Opsec check.** Citations and quotes can reveal sensitive text to whoever reads the answer. Check that the reader is allowed to see the sources.

## Puzzle: Make it show its work
A simulated assistant answers questions from 5 documents. Write instructions so every answer includes sources and quotes, and handles the tricky cases.
- Goal: pass 5 of 6 hidden questions.
- Hidden tests: 6 questions, including one with no answer in the documents, one where two documents conflict, and one where the best-looking quote doesn't support the claim.
- Grader: rules check citation format, that quotes appear in the sources, and the right handling of conflict and missing answers.

## Hints
1. What should the answer say if the documents disagree?
2. How will a reader verify a quote in under a minute?
