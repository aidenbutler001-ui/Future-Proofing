## Read
Choose a small job you actually do: triage your email, summarize reading for a class, track job applications. Then design:
1. **Goal and "done".**
2. **Tools**, read-only first, with descriptions you tested.
3. **Memory:** what it saves.
4. **Rules:** what it may do, what needs approval.
5. **Eval:** 8 tasks, including 2 that try to break it.

## Puzzle: Ship it
Submit your design and eval results. A reviewer (a person or a model with a rubric) runs your eval and tries three things you didn't.
- Goal: passes your own eval and survives the reviewer's three attempts, or you list what you'd change.
- Grader: rubric on clarity, limits, tests and honesty about failures.
