## Read
Sometimes the material is too long to fit, or it fits but the model misses things buried in the middle. Options:

1. **Cut to what matters.** Often the best fix. Paste the relevant chapter, not the book.
2. **Split and combine (map-reduce).** Summarize each section with the same instructions, then summarize the summaries. Fast, but details can be lost at each stage, so say what to keep ("all dates and amounts").
3. **Ask in passes.** First ask which sections matter, then work on those.
4. **Search instead of reading everything** (lesson 3.3).

Pitfalls:
- A summary of a summary can lose or distort facts.
- Models can be less reliable on details in the middle of very long inputs.
- Always note which parts were left out so you know what the answer doesn't cover.

**Opsec check.** Splitting and summarizing sends every piece to the model, and summaries of sensitive documents are themselves sensitive. Don't forward summaries somewhere the original couldn't go.

## Puzzle: The 40-page report
A simulated 40-page report is split into 8 sections, with a budget of 3 model calls. Your job is to answer 3 hidden questions that depend on facts in different sections. Choose how to split, what to ask for in each summary, and how to combine.
- Goal: all 3 questions answered correctly within budget.
- Hidden tests: the 3 questions; one relies on a number that is easy to drop in a summary.
- Grader: rules check the answers and the call count; the trace shows where a fact was lost.

## Hints
1. What must each summary keep so the next step can use it?
2. Which sections can you skip for each question?
